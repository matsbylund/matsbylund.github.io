Harmonic measure and Julia sets

We study harmonic measure on the Julia set $J_c$ of a quadratic polynomial $f_c(z) = z^2 + c$.

In particular, for $\omega_c$-a.e. $z \in J_c$ we show \[ \lim_{r \to 0} \frac{\log \omega_c(B(z,r))}{\log r} = \frac{1}{2}. \]
